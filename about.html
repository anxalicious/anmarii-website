About Mazely

Mazely is a fictional educational project that makes cybersecurity learning approachable through interactive, story-driven challenges.

Our Educational Purpose

Mazely is designed to help learners build practical digital safety habits. Its game concepts introduce topics such as recognizing phishing, protecting accounts, managing social media privacy, and checking online information.

Learn by Exploring

Rather than presenting cybersecurity as a list of rules, Mazely places each lesson in a familiar online scenario. Learners can consider their options, follow clues, and reflect on how small decisions can affect their safety and privacy.

Designed for Everyday Digital Life

The activities are intended for people who want to feel more confident online, including learners who are new to cybersecurity. The focus is on clear explanations and useful awareness, not technical prerequisites.

Project Status

Mazely is a fictional educational project, and its interactive games are currently in development.
