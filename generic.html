Challenges

Explore four interactive game concepts that turn everyday online situations into practical cybersecurity lessons. Each challenge focuses on skills you can use to make safer decisions online.

Phish or Fiction?

Difficulty:
Duration: 15-20 min
Age: 10+
Format: Quiz

Step into a simulated inbox and decide which messages deserve your trust. Look beyond a familiar-looking logo and inspect the sender, the language, and what each message is asking you to do.

Practice spotting urgent requests, unexpected attachments, and links that may lead somewhere different from what they claim. The challenge builds a habit of pausing and verifying a message through a trusted channel before responding.

The Password Vault

Difficulty:
Duration: 20-25 min
Age: 12+
Format: Puzzle

Protect a set of accounts by choosing how to create and manage credentials. Explore why long, unique passwords or passphrases are safer than reusing a simple password across multiple sites.

You'll also learn how a password manager can help with unique credentials and how two-factor authentication adds another layer of protection if a password is exposed.

Think Before You Share

Difficulty:
Duration: 10-15 min
Age: 11+
Format: Scenario

Explore a social media scenario and consider who can see a post, what personal details it reveals, and how long that information might remain available. Your choices help uncover the privacy trade-offs behind everyday sharing.

Practice reviewing audience and location settings, limiting unnecessary personal details, and thinking about the effect a post could have on you or someone else.

The Digital Detective

Difficulty:
Duration: 25-30 min
Age: 13+
Format: Escape Room

Investigate an online claim by checking where it came from, who published it, and when it was shared. Learn to notice missing context, emotional headlines, and other signs that a post may be misleading.

Build a simple verification routine: compare independent reliable sources, check the original context, and separate evidence from opinion before sharing information.
